Privacy

Who we are

Gleaner is a browser extension by Spolora that helps you save English words, phrases, and sentences while you read, then review them in a side panel and export them to Anki.

What we collect

On your device (default)

When you save a selection, Gleaner stores on your computer:

  • the selected text
  • surrounding sentence context (when available)
  • page title and page URL
  • dictionary content you looked up (definitions, phonetics, examples, glosses, etymology when available)
  • optional notes you write
  • review metadata (for example created time)
  • cached pronunciation audio for saved words
  • a bundled offline English lexicon (loaded on your device; no account required)
  • downloaded native-gloss dictionary packs (Chinese / Japanese / Korean and other supported languages), when needed
  • optional local vocab-level test results
  • short translation cache entries (so repeated glosses do not need another network call)

This local data stays in the browser until you clear it or uninstall Gleaner.

If you sign in
Optional Google sign-in syncs your cards and latest vocab-level summary to our cloud database (Supabase), tied to your account (email / user id from Google). You can keep using Gleaner without signing in; then that data stays local only.

Signed-in users may also use a limited daily machine-translation quota (Google Gemini, via our Supabase Edge Function). Only the selected word or phrase (and the target language / intent) is sent for that request—not your browsing history.

Anonymous community map (optional)
To show a shared “learners on Earth” map on our website, the extension may send at most once per day: a country code only (derived from your timezone, not GPS and not your IP). We do not store a user id with that ping. Counts are aggregated by country and day. You can turn this off in Settings → Community map. The map never shows cities or individuals. The website globe may open facing your region using the same timezone guess — still not GPS.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your cards for advertising.

Data sent over the network

Common English lookups are served from the offline lexicon on your device. When a word is missing, or for etymology / translation / images, Gleaner may send the selected word or phrase (and similar short queries) to third-party services:

Purpose Provider examples
Offline English definitions (on device) Bundled lexicon built from English Wiktionary (Kaikki / Wiktextract)
Online English definitions / etymology (fallback) Wiktionary, Datamuse
Proper-name / place summaries (fallback) Wikipedia
Native word gloss (on device) CC-CEDICT (MDBG), JMdict (GitHub), Kengdic (GitHub), Wikdict TEI packs, etc.
Phrase / sentence / miss translation; pronunciation audio Youdao; for signed-in users, Google Gemini via our Supabase function (daily limit)
Optional mnemonic images Wikimedia / Wikidata / Wikipedia
Account & sync Google (sign-in), Supabase (cards & vocab level)
Anonymous country presence (optional) Supabase (country code + day count only)

We do not intentionally send your full browsing history. Lookups happen when you select text or open related features.

Permissions

Gleaner asks for access needed to read the current page selection, show the side panel, store settings, and (where available) complete sign-in. Host access is used so dictionary and sync requests can run from the extension.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data: remove it by clearing extension storage or uninstalling Gleaner.
  • Cloud cards / vocab profile: sign in and delete in the product, or contact us to request account-related deletion.
  • Community map counts are anonymous aggregates and are not tied to an account.
  • Third-party services keep data under their own policies.

Children

Gleaner is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it if you are under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: Open an issue on GitHub.

隐私

我们是谁

Gleaner 是 Spolora 推出的浏览器扩展：你在阅读时划选英文单词、短语或句子并收藏，在侧边栏复习，并可导出到 Anki

我们收集什么

默认仅保存在本机

当你收藏内容时，Gleaner 会在你的电脑/浏览器中保存：

  • 划选的文本
  • 上下文句子（如能提取）
  • 页面标题与网址
  • 查阅到的词典信息（释义、音标、例句、母语释义、词源等）
  • 你填写的笔记
  • 复习相关时间信息
  • 已收藏单词的发音缓存
  • 随扩展附带的离线英语词库（在本机加载，无需登录）
  • 按需下载的母语词典包（中文 / 日文 / 韩文及其他已支持语言）
  • 可选的词汇水平测试结果（本机）
  • 简短的翻译缓存（避免重复请求）

在你清空扩展数据或卸载之前，这些内容留在本机

如果你选择登录
可选的 Google 登录会把卡片与最近一次词汇水平摘要同步到我们的云端数据库（Supabase），并与你的账号（来自 Google 的邮箱 / 用户 ID）关联。不登录也可以正常使用，这些内容只留在本机

登录用户还可使用有限的每日机翻额度（Google Gemini，经由我们的 Supabase Edge Function）。此类请求仅发送你划选的词或短语（以及目标语言 / 用途），不会上传浏览历史

匿名社区地图（可选）
为在官网展示「地球上的学习者」地图，扩展最多每天发送一次：仅国家代码（由时区粗略推断，不是 GPS，也不使用你的 IP）。我们不会把用户 ID 与该心跳一起存储；按国家与日期聚合计数。可在设置中关闭「社区地图」。地图不会显示城市或个人。官网地球也可能按同一时区猜测转到你所在地区——同样不是 GPS

我们不出售你的数据，也不把卡片用于广告

会发到网络上的内容

常见英文释义优先使用本机离线词库。词库未收录，或需要词源 / 翻译 / 配图时，扩展可能把你划选的词或短语（及类似短查询）发送给第三方服务：

用途 服务示例
离线英文释义（本机） 随包装载的英语维基词典精简词库（Kaikki / Wiktextract）
在线英文释义 / 词源（兜底） Wiktionary、Datamuse
人名 / 地名摘要（兜底） 维基百科
单词母语释义（本机） CC-CEDICT（MDBG）、JMdict（GitHub）、Kengdic（GitHub）、Wikdict 等
短语 / 句子 / 未收录词翻译；发音音频 有道；登录用户可通过我们的后端使用 Google Gemini（有每日上限）
可选助记图片 Wikimedia / Wikidata / Wikipedia
账号与同步 Google（登录）、Supabase（卡片与词汇水平）
匿名国家活跃（可选） Supabase（仅国家码 + 日计数）

我们不会故意上传你的完整浏览历史。请求发生在你划词或使用相关功能时

权限说明

扩展需要读取当前页的选中文本、打开侧边栏、保存设置，并在支持的浏览器中完成登录。主机访问权限用于词典查询与同步请求

保存与删除

  • 本机数据：清空扩展存储，或卸载 Gleaner
  • 云端卡片 / 词汇水平：登录后在产品内删除，或联系我们处理账号相关删除
  • 社区地图计数为匿名聚合，不绑定账号
  • 第三方服务按其各自政策处理数据
