Much of good reporting is serendipity: the right question asked at the right door. Writers chase clarity, not esoteric jargon, and still leave room for nuance.

Every author has an idiosyncratic rhythm. Online praise can feel ephemeral, but careful reading compounds. Good interfaces lower cognitive friction without flattening ideas into slogans.

As a rule of thumb, revise once after you think you are done. Reform that only protects the status quo rarely helps readers grow.
